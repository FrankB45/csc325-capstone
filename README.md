**Project Idea #1: Git Project Explorer** 
**Description:** Web app that accepts a URL to a github repo (must be web app)  and produces a timeline view of the development of the project and how the web app looked, commit-by-commit.

**Project Idea #2: Planner with Automatic Schedule Modification**
**Description:** A smart daily planner that allows users to create and organize their daily routines, appointments, meetings, workouts, study sessions, and other activities. Users can assign a priority level to each event and specify how much preparation or travel time is needed. The planner automatically adjusts the user's schedule when a new event is added or when an existing event changes. It considers event priority, duration, preparation time, travel time, and conflicts to create the most efficient schedule possible. For example, if a user has a non-priority gym session scheduled from 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM and then adds a meeting from 8:00 AM to 9:30 AM with 20 minutes of travel time, the planner can automatically move the gym session to 10:30 AM–12:30 PM rather than simply marking the events as conflicting. The goal is to help users spend less time manually reorganizing their schedules and more time focusing on their priorities.

**Project Idea #3: Internship Navigator with AI interview Assistant**
**Description:** A web application that aids college students in organizing their internship applications and prepare for interviews.

**Project Idea #4: DriveReady**
**Description:** My idea is to create an app called DriveReady that helps people check their driving readiness by testing things like reaction time, attention, memory, and decision-making. The goal is to identify temporary changes that could make driving less safe, without assuming that having a mental illness automatically makes someone unable to drive.

**Project Idea #5: AI Cooking Assistant**
**Description:** With this app, users will first be able to upload their favorite recipes. When they are ready to begin a recipe they enter into a hands-free conversation with a personal AI cooking assistant who will guide them through each step. The AI can answer recipe-related questions, repeat steps and measurements needed. It can tell you what to start prepping next while you wait for your water to boil.
